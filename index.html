Rules for 2-credit electives (from the Dean Academics note, 25 Oct 2026)
  • A 2-credit elective is 20 hours of class: 10 sessions of 2 hours, held over 2 to 5 weeks. It also includes at least one small group discussion (3 students or fewer) with the faculty.
  • You may register for not more than one 2-credit elective in a trimester, and you must enrol in at least 14 credits in every term.
  • You may take not more than two 2-credit electives in an academic year, including 2-credit clinic courses, and must enrol in at least 48 credits in the year (corrected from 64 by AAD).
  • If you opt for a 2-credit clinic, you may register for only one additional 2-credit clinic in that trimester.
  • 2-credit electives are evaluated under the same continuous evaluation framework as 4-credit electives, including written work tied to the small group discussions.
  • Clashes in this planner take course dates into account: two courses in the same weekly slot only clash if their dates overlap. Courses without announced dates are treated as running all trimester.
